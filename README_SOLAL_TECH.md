# Building and Leading a LegalTech Venture

### From Market Discovery to Product Development, Fundraising and B2B Commercialization

**Company:** SOLAL TECH  
**Industry:** LegalTech / SaaS / Digital Assets  
**Role:** Founder & CEO  
**Period:** 2019–2024, including initial concept development  
**Market:** France, with international business development initiatives

---

## Executive Summary

I founded SOLAL TECH to address an emerging challenge at the intersection of technology, wealth management and inheritance: how can individuals organize, secure and transmit their digital assets and online identity after death?

Existing options were fragmented across account-specific procedures, digital vaults and estate-planning services. I explored how a more coordinated experience could connect individuals, legal professionals and beneficiaries.

What began as a consumer-focused concept evolved into a LegalTech venture developing a digital inheritance platform and a professional SaaS offering for the notarial sector.

As Founder & CEO, I was responsible for building and leading the business across product strategy, technology delivery, marketing, brand development, communications, fundraising, investor relations, business development and organizational management.

## 1. Opportunity Discovery and Market Research

The idea for SOLAL TECH originated from an observation about the growing importance of digital identity and assets in people's lives.

Social media accounts, online services, cloud storage and cryptocurrencies had become part of personal and financial estates, yet managing their transmission after death remained complicated.

I identified challenges for three distinct audiences: individuals wishing to organize their digital legacy, beneficiaries dealing with the deceased's online accounts, and legal professionals responsible for supporting inheritance processes.

Before moving into development, I conducted market research through consumer surveys, professional interviews, competitor analysis and digital acquisition experiments.

An early landing-page campaign generated more than **2,500 expressions of interest**, while subsequent research collected hundreds of consumer responses.

I also engaged with notaries and insurance professionals to understand existing practices, identify potential distribution channels and assess the operational relevance of the proposed solution.

These findings influenced the business model. The original direct-to-consumer approach expanded into B2B and B2B2C opportunities involving trusted professional intermediaries.

The central challenge was not simply generating consumer interest. It was developing a proposition that could address different stakeholder needs within a workable commercial model.

## 2. Product Strategy and Platform Development

Building the product was one of my core responsibilities as Founder & CEO.

I translated market research into a product vision, functional specifications and development priorities, working with UX/UI designers, product specialists and technical teams.

The platform was conceived around three interconnected user experiences:

- **Individuals:** organize their digital inventory, record instructions and designate beneficiaries.
- **Notaries:** access professional workflows supporting clients with digital inheritance matters.
- **Beneficiaries:** obtain relevant information and assistance with digital estate processes.

Designing these journeys required balancing usability, security, legal considerations and the different responsibilities of each participant.

### From Concept to MVP

I coordinated the development process from the business side, including:

- Product vision and functional requirements.
- User journeys, wireframes and UX/UI reviews.
- Feature prioritization and roadmap planning.
- Technical specifications and collaboration with developers.
- Coordination of development sprints, budgets and external providers.
- Alignment between product delivery and commercial requirements.

An early consumer MVP was completed in 2022, followed by continued development of the broader platform and its professional proposition.

I was responsible for the business direction and coordination of product development, while specialist contributors handled software engineering and technical architecture.

### Website Development and Digital Experience

Alongside the application, I oversaw the creation and evolution of the SOLAL TECH website.

The website was a central part of the customer journey: it introduced the problem, communicated the product's value, supported acquisition campaigns and generated registrations or commercial inquiries.

I coordinated its positioning, content, design and conversion objectives, connecting the digital experience with the broader brand and marketing strategy.

As the commercial model evolved, product and website messaging were adapted to address the expectations of legal professionals alongside those of individual users.

This experience strengthened my ability to connect customer insights, product decisions, technical resources and go-to-market priorities.

## 3. Brand, Marketing and Communications

Creating SOLAL TECH required building awareness around an emerging subject while establishing credibility with consumers, legal professionals, investors and institutional stakeholders.

I led the company's marketing and communications strategy, from brand development and the initial website to acquisition campaigns, public relations and executive communications.

### Brand Positioning and Marketing Strategy

I developed the company's positioning and messaging, coordinating visual identity work, marketing materials and audience-specific communication.

The challenge was to make a complex legal and technological proposition understandable without oversimplifying the problem.

Marketing initiatives included:

- Website and landing-page development.
- SEO, educational articles and content marketing.
- Paid acquisition and lead-generation campaigns.
- LinkedIn prospecting and professional outreach.
- Email marketing and nurturing sequences.
- Webinars with professional contributors.
- Product demonstrations and conversion initiatives.
- Customer engagement, retention and upselling campaigns.

The approach evolved with the product.

Initial campaigns focused on awareness and testing interest in the problem and proposed solution. Later activities increasingly targeted qualified professional conversations, SaaS adoption and customer engagement.

### Educational Marketing

Webinars and specialist content played an important role because digital inheritance was not yet an established purchasing category for many potential customers.

I developed educational initiatives to explain the risks of unmanaged digital assets, demonstrate professional use cases and generate engagement with the company's offering.

These activities connected thought leadership with commercial development, particularly in the notarial market, where professional credibility and trust were essential.

### Public Relations and Corporate Communications

I was also responsible for building the company's external visibility and managing its corporate narrative.

This included preparing press releases, company presentations, editorial materials, investor communications and announcements around major business milestones.

In July 2022, SOLAL TECH issued a press release announcing its financing and presenting its technology, professional positioning and development ambitions.

I represented the company as its founder and spokesperson in discussions with journalists, partners, professional audiences and entrepreneurial networks.

The objective was to establish SOLAL TECH as a credible emerging LegalTech business while supporting acquisition, partnerships and investor confidence.

## 5. Team Leadership and Delivery Coordination

At its largest, SOLAL TECH mobilized a **14-person cross-functional organization**, combining internal contributors and external specialists.

The team brought together expertise in product management, engineering, UX/UI, marketing, business development, legal matters and operations.

As Founder & CEO, I was responsible for setting the overall direction, defining priorities and coordinating execution across these functions.

My management responsibilities included recruitment and onboarding coordination, resource allocation, project oversight, cross-functional meetings and follow-up on operational objectives.

A recurring challenge was aligning teams with different priorities.

Technical development needed to remain consistent with available budgets and product requirements. Marketing and sales needed reliable information about functionality and launch readiness. External specialists needed clear briefs, deliverables and decision-making processes.

I worked closely with product owners, developers, designers and commercial contributors to connect these priorities and make trade-offs when necessary.

Managing a combination of internal and external resources also required close attention to collaboration, delivery timelines and partner relationships.

This experience required me to coordinate product, finance, marketing, technology and commercial execution as interdependent parts of the same business.

## 6. B2B Commercialization and International Exploration

### Developing the Professional Market

While the initial product concept addressed individual consumers, SOLAL TECH increasingly focused its commercial efforts on the notarial sector.

I developed professional relationships, conducted market discussions and helped shape the SaaS proposition around the needs of legal professionals.

The offering evolved to include a professional subscription, educational resources and complementary services related to digital inheritance.

Commercial activities combined direct outreach, product demonstrations, professional webinars, partner engagement and follow-up campaigns.

By 2024, investor materials reported **10 paying notary customers**, alongside a broader network of professional contacts and ongoing commercial discussions.

This represented early commercial traction, not a scaled or financially sustainable SaaS business.

### Partnerships

I developed relationships across the notarial, legal, insurance, wealth-management and technology sectors.

These discussions supported customer discovery, product positioning, potential distribution and professional credibility.

The work required adapting the company's proposition to different stakeholders, while recognizing that a positive relationship did not automatically translate into a commercial agreement.

### International Business Development

I also represented SOLAL TECH beyond France, traveling to **Switzerland, Luxembourg and Canada** to present the venture and explore opportunities.

These initiatives involved business discussions, networking and market exploration with international professional and entrepreneurial audiences.

They provided valuable experience in presenting a technology proposition across different markets, evaluating potential distribution models and adapting communications to different professional environments.

These activities were exploratory rather than completed international commercial launches.

---

## 7. Business Outcome and Lessons

Following two financing rounds, the company continued developing its technology and pursuing commercial adoption.

Further capital was required to sustain the next phase of development. Despite additional fundraising efforts, the necessary financing was not secured.

**SOLAL TECH did not reach financial sustainability and was liquidated in October 2024.**

The experience reinforced several principles that continue to shape my work:

- **Market validation:** early interest, positive feedback and partnerships must ultimately translate into repeatable paying demand.
- **Product strategy:** customer needs, technical execution and commercial incentives need to evolve together.
- **Financial discipline:** product ambition and growth plans must remain aligned with available funding and runway.
- **Leadership:** building a company requires constant coordination between strategy, people, resources and execution.
- **Adaptability:** early assumptions must be challenged as customer feedback and market conditions evolve.

### Closing Reflection

Building SOLAL TECH gave me firsthand experience of connecting market discovery, product development, financing and commercial execution under resource constraints. That experience continues to shape how I approach product strategy, marketing leadership and cross-functional decision-making.
